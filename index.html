Our mission

The Galaxy Evolution Vertically Integrated Projects (GEVIP) program is modeled after the Vertically Integrated Projects program at Georgia Tech. It brings together students from astronomy, physics, and related fields to research galaxy evolution for course credit, with mentoring from a PI, postdocs, graduate students, and senior undergraduates.

Unlike a typical course-based research class, every capstone project is conceived by the student. GEVIP students get the full experience of being a scientist — reading the literature, building the analysis, and defending the result.

How the program runs

Three years, one research question of your own.

  1. 01

    Spring, sophomore year — AST 211G

    Training modules

    Students work through astronomical data analysis in Python and start reading papers to find the questions that interest them.

  2. 02

    Fall, junior year — AST 212G

    Proposal

    Each student writes a proposal for their own capstone project, scoped to real datasets available to the group.

  3. 03

    Final three semesters — AST 222G / 322G

    Capstone research

    Students carry out the project, primarily using James Webb Space Telescope data, and present the result.
